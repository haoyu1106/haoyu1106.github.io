Research

  • DNA damage and chemical protection

    I study DNA damage caused by ionizing radiation and atmospheric-pressure plasma, and how the chemical environment and protective compounds alter these effects.

  • Electron-induced surface chemistry

    Using XPS, I examine how low-energy electrons change the chemistry of DNA films and fluoropolymer surfaces.

  • Fluoropolymer decomposition

    My published work examines how electron-beam irradiation, temperature, and catalysts affect the bulk decomposition of PTFE.
